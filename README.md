# Respirator - a portable life support machine

This is a project me and my friends were working on during our time at Lublin University of Technology. It has earned us the title of Bachelors of Engineering and was the subject of our thesis.\
The aim of the project is to someday save lives in hard to reach places.

## My contribution

I led the project and was primarily responsible for the physical prototype and
the STM32 software. My work included:

- developing the STM32 software and algorithms
- integrating the hardware and software components (motor control, sensors)
- coordinating the project direction and technical decisions.
- creating and building the custom enclosure

## Status

This repository contains the academic prototype. It is not maintained as a
production medical device.
